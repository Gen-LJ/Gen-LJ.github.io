Hail, traveller! I'm Kaung, a Senior Mobile Developer from Yangon, Myanmar, forging apps for Android and iOS.

For more than three years I've shipped Flutter apps for HR, lending, e-commerce, internet service and streaming. Today I lead a four-person mobile team at UMG Group, across Flutter and native Android (Kotlin). Six of my apps are live on Google Play and the App Store, including [ShweStream] with more than 50,000 downloads.

My strengths are Clean Architecture with BLoC, payment integration and mentoring. Outside the day job I built [SurveyNext], a full-stack survey platform: a Go REST API with native Android (Jetpack Compose) and iOS (SwiftUI) apps.

SurveyNext

Elite quest · Go · Kotlin · SwiftUI

A survey platform with three roles. Interviewers build, draft, publish and analyse surveys. Respondents discover and answer them, keep a history and earn points. Admins keep the realm in order.

Objectives

  • Forge the backend: a Go REST API on Gin and GORM with TiDB Cloud (MySQL), JWT auth, role-based routes, pagination, and unit and route tests, deployed as a distroless Docker image on Render.
  • Raise the Android app: Kotlin and Jetpack Compose (Material 3), MVVM with Hilt, StateFlow and coroutines, and Retrofit with an auth interceptor.
  • Cross to iOS: migrated to native SwiftUI with @Observable MVVM, async/await, Keychain sessions and no third-party dependencies.

Rewards

You will receive:

  • Go · Gin · GORM
  • Kotlin · Compose
  • SwiftUI
  • Docker on Render
